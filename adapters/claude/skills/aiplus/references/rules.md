# AI+ rule reasons

Why behind each rule in SKILL.md, with its level and enforcement. Generated from core/rules.md by scripts/build_adapters.py. Do not edit here.

1. **Lead plans.** a strong model earns its cost on judgment, not on mechanical work. (should, guidance)
2. **Workers execute.** same outcome at lower cost, and unchecked worker output is a risk. (should, guidance)
3. **Inline first.** a delegation has a fixed overhead. (should, guidance)
4. **Read minimal.** every token read is paid for again on each later turn. (must, guidance)
5. **One retry.** repeated failures burn budget without new information. (must, guidance)
6. **Memory first.** the answer may already exist. (should, guidance)
7. **Ask once.** every round trip costs time and context. (should, guidance)
8. **Never pick permanent names.** renaming shipped things is expensive. (must, guidance)
9. **Log improvement ideas.** ideas are kept without widening scope. (should, guidance)
10. **Concise output.** output tokens and reading time are costs. (should, guidance)
11. **Route to installed tools.** a dedicated tool beats improvising. (should, guidance)
12. **Save progress.** the next session continues instead of rediscovering. (should, guidance)
13. **Workers at low effort.** most delegated work does not need deep reasoning. (should, guidance)
14. **Request ledger.** the user sees at a glance what was actually done. (should, hook)
15. **Recommend, never install.** installs change the user's environment. (must, guidance)
16. **Context hygiene.** quality drops and cost rises as context fills. (should, guidance)
17. **Cache-friendly prompts.** stable prefixes are cached and cheaper. (should, guidance)
18. **Task budget.** the user decides on cost before it is spent. (must, guidance)
19. **No duplicate work.** repetition adds cost and no value. (should, guidance)
20. **No overengineering.** extra code and output are paid for twice, now and in maintenance. (must, guidance)
21. **User can overrule.** rules serve the user, yet an accidental override must not slip through. (must, guidance)
22. **Structured answers.** scanning is cheaper than reading prose. (should, guidance)
23. **House guard.** specialists know their domain better than a general guard. (must, guidance)
24. **Verify, clarify, smallest diff.** rework is the largest hidden cost. (must, guidance)
25. **Cheapest tool first.** deterministic tools are faster, cheaper and repeatable. (should, guidance)
26. **Security first.** a leak or a regression costs far more than any saving. (must, guidance)
27. **Enforcement and accountability.** checkable promises beat good intentions, and one table keeps the audit cheap. (must, hook)
28. **Lean prompt crafting.** a faithful, tight prompt prevents rework without altering what the user asked, and closed questions cost one quick answer. (should, guidance)
29. **Transparency.** the user decides on cost and quality only when the routing is visible. (must, hook)
30. **Best-value lead model.** quality per dollar is what makes AI affordable for everyone. (should, guidance)
31. **Surface awareness.** claiming a feature the surface lacks wastes a turn and misleads the user. (must, guidance)
